Mentorship Through Content Creation

At its core, architecture only works in collaborative practice, and I strongly believe mentorship is empirical. This personal project aims to help those eager to learn the leading architectural modeling software that moves the working field forward: Revit. Through a case study of one of the most influential buildings of the 20th century, Villa Savoye, I teach the fundamentals of Revit's UI for basic modeling and architectural representation. Two courses consisting of 28+ episodes, analyzing and executing the basics of 3d software modeling and post-production detailing with Illustrator and Photoshop.

Beginner Level Architectural Representation and Modeling Tutorials

While introducing students to 3d modeling softwares, this study also explores architectural representation through post-production, using Photoshop & Illustrator to communicate materiality, light, scale, and visual depth for resilient architectural representation.
